# Parameter Experimentation
Here are the four variations for our carpenter story:
1. Temp = 0.1 + Top-p = 1.0 (The Reliable Professional)

Focus: Precision and logical progression.

The master carpenter smoothed the surface of the oak table until it felt like silk under his palm. He had spent weeks carving the intricate joints, ensuring that every piece fit together with mathematical precision. As the sun set, he wiped away the sawdust and stepped back to admire a piece of furniture built to last for generations.
2. Temp = 1.0 + Top-p = 1.0 (The Drunken Poet)

Focus: Maximum randomness, obscure metaphors, and risky word choices.

Elias didn’t just prune cedar; he exhaled mahogany splinters and sang to the ancient, resinous rings trapped in amber time. His chisel performed a frantic, jagged waltz across the grain, unearthing a knot that looked suspiciously like a weeping eye or a map to a sunken archipelago. By midnight, the sideboard wasn't just furniture—it was a wooden lung breathing the scent of a thousand storms.
3. Temp = 0.1 + Top-p = 0.1 (The Safe Robot)

Focus: Minimalist, repetitive, and extremely predictable.

The carpenter worked on the wood in his shop. He used his tools to make a table. He sanded the wood until it was smooth. He was happy with the work he had done. The table was strong and it was ready to be used.
4. Temp = 1.0 + Top-p = 0.1 (The Confident Bore)

Focus: Randomized selection from a very tiny, safe list of words.

The carpenter worked on the wood in his shop. He used his tools to make a table. He sanded the wood until it was smooth. He was happy with the work he had done. The table was strong and it was ready to be used.
The Science Behind the Results

The fourth setting (Temp 1.0 / Top-p 0.1) is the most interesting to analyze. You might notice it looks almost identical to the third one. Here’s why:

    Top-p at 0.1 is like a "VIP list." It tells the AI: "I don't care how many words you know; you can only look at the top 10% of the most likely words."

    Temperature at 1.0 tells the AI: "Pick a word from your list randomly, don't just pick the #1 best one."

    The Result: Because the list of allowed words is so small (due to the low Top-p), the AI is "shuffling" a deck of only 5 or 10 very basic words. Even though it's trying to be "random," it has no interesting words to choose from, so it still sounds repetitive and simple.
